About Me

Hey! I’m Henry Huang. Originally from Shanghai, China, I'm currently based in Dallas, TX, pursuing my Bachelor’s in Electrical and Computer Engineering at SMU, with a minor in Music (Saxophone Performance).

I believe in the intersection of technology and art. Whether I'm analyzing communication protocols, fixing a 1995 Miata ECU, or performing with the Mustang Band, I love figuring out how things work and making them better.

Music and engineering may seem like different worlds, but to me, they are deeply connected. Both are about listening closely, understanding complex systems, and turning ideas into something others can experience. That intersection continues to shape how I create, perform, and solve problems.

I’m also passionate about aviation and the freedom of flight. I love traveling—not only to discover new places, but also to visit friends wherever life has taken them. Every journey gives me a new perspective and reminds me how engineering can bring people and places closer together.

> Interests:
Aviation, Travel, Cycling, Music, Mechanical Engineering, J-Pop, Open Source, 3D Printing.

AUG 2025 — MAY 2026

Automotive Radar

77 GHz FMCW RADAR · ADAS · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

A low-cost forward collision warning system built around TI’s AWR2944P mmWave radar. The project looks at what it takes to turn raw radar data into something useful on the road: tracking distance and relative speed, deciding which target actually matters, and eventually tying that into a small automatic braking demo.

SUMMER 2026

Industrial Automation Internship

BECKHOFF AUTOMATION · TWINCAT · INDUSTRIAL CONTROL

At Beckhoff Automation, I worked across industrial AI, machine vision, and multi-axis motion control—exploring how software, PLC logic, sensing, and physical machinery come together as complete automation systems.

Industrial AI Agent
LLM · MCP · TWINCAT · ENGINEERING AUTOMATION

I experimented with how AI agents can work inside a real industrial engineering environment instead of just answering questions in a chat box. The work revolved around TwinCAT, MCP tools, PLC projects, System Manager, and how an agent can inspect, understand, and safely work with an automation project.

Machine Vision
INDUSTRIAL VISION · MOTION CONTROL · PLC

This started as a vision task and quickly became a much bigger controls problem. Cameras, contour detection, calibration, conveyors, robot motion, and a rotary cutter all had to agree on where an object was and what should happen next. Much of the work involved getting those pieces to behave like one system.

Writing Robot
G-CODE · TWINCAT · ADS · MOTION CONTROL

One of my first larger TwinCAT motion projects was a robot that could draw from G-code. I used Python ADS communication to send motion data into the PLC and spent quite a bit of time dealing with buffers, machine states, pen-up/pen-down logic, and all the little things that show up once a motion demo has to run more than once.

Delta Robot
MULTI-AXIS MOTION · KINEMATICS · PLC

The Delta robot was where TwinCAT motion control started getting properly complicated. Three linked robot axes, an extra rotary axis, virtual Cartesian coordinates, conveyors, and coupled motion made it a good exercise in understanding how a multi-axis machine is actually organized rather than just commanding one motor at a time.

During my time in the SMU Physics Lab, I worked on automating optical-fiber reliability testing for the ATLAS project. Python scripts handled a bench full of lab equipment over VISA, serial, and USB connections, which made repetitive measurement runs much less painful and much more consistent.
